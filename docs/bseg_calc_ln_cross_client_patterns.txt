CI_BSEG_CALC_LN — Cross-Client Pattern Note (read-only)
=======================================================
As of 2026-08-03. Frozen bill segments, roughly last 3–6 months.
Clients checked live: CityCorp (deep), Ellensburg, Odessa DEV,
College Station, Fond du Lac. Newark failed mid-query (ORA-03135
connection lost).

What is the same everywhere
---------------------------
- Grain is always one row per calc line under a calc header
  (BSEG_ID + HEADER_SEQ + SEQNO).
- Amounts live on CALC_AMT; billable determinant on BILL_SQ.
- UOM / SQI / TOU may be blank on some lines depending on rate design.
- PRT_SW and APP_IN_SUMM_SW control bill-print vs summary behavior.
- Mixing Calc Line with BSEG_SQ or BSEG_READ in Ad Hoc still fans out
  on every client — that domain rule does not change by client.


What is different (and why CityCorp looks “weird”)
--------------------------------------------------
CityCorp is NOT averaging usage into tiny pieces.

It is a water / sewer / sanitation rate design that writes MANY
bill-presentation lines per segment: meter charges, tier usage,
“Total …” rollups, franchise fees, and tax lines. Most of those lines
have no UOM and no SQI. Only the real consumption lines carry
Thousand Gallons + T1/T2/T3 Multiplier.

Example CityCorp residential water segment (10 lines):

  1  Meter Charge - 5/8                         $14.15   print Y  (no UOM/SQI)
  2  Water - 1 TGAL at $2.60                    $2.60    print Y  Thousand Gallons / T1 Multiplier
  3  Total Water Consumption Charge             $2.60    print Y  (no UOM/SQI)  <-- rollup
  4  Fed Safe Drinking Water Act                $0.40    print Y  (no UOM/SQI)
  5  Sanitation Charges subject to tax          $3.47    print N  (no UOM/SQI)
  6  Franchise Fee                              $0.75    print Y  (no UOM/SQI)
  7  City Sales Tax                             $0.31    print N  app_in_summ Y
  8  County Sales Tax                           $0.21    print N  app_in_summ Y
  9  State Sales Tax                            $1.36    print N  app_in_summ Y
 10  Total Taxes                                $1.88    print Y  (no UOM/SQI)  <-- rollup

So the “tiny amounts” are mostly fees/taxes/cents, not fractional gallons.
The “ton of calc lines” is print/summary structure, not one line per gallon.

Reporting guidance
------------------
Same rules for all clients:
1. Stay in Calc Line folder for charge-line analysis.
2. Do not mix BSEG_SQ / BSEG_READ into the same Ad Hoc.
3. Filter by client-specific UOM/SQI patterns — they are not portable.

Client-specific:
- CityCorp water usage: filter Calc Line UOM = Thousand Gallons
  and/or SQI like T1/T2/T3 Multiplier.
  For bill-facing dollars: prefer PRT_SW = Y, and be careful summing
  both detail and “Total …” rollup lines (double count risk).
- CityCorp taxes: often Print N + App In Summary Y, then a Print Y
  “Total Taxes” rollup.
- Ellensburg water: calc lines usually have no UOM; use SQI
  (Days, Tier Multiplier). Gallons live in Service Quantity (BSEG_SQ).
- Do not assume “one calc line = one commodity unit” on any client.
