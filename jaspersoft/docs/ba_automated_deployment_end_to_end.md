# OriginBA automated deployment, end to end (2026-09-28)

The written companion of `OriginBA_Automated_Deployment.pptx` (built from `deployment_deck/spec.json` by
the `originba-process-deck` skill). For a developer joining the product: what moves data from a
client's C2M database to their Jaspersoft reports, what runs on its own, what runs on instruction,
what each step proves, and where it still needs work. Every path is real; measured numbers carry
their date.

## The shape

```
Client C2M (Oracle, CISADM, read-only)
   |-- legacy, in production today: REFRESH_* procedures -> CISADM.*_RPT_CURR + CMS_SA_SNAPSHOT ("active 8")
   |-- in-database dbt warehouse (INT_DEV bench, Ellensburg test): ORIGINBA_STAGING -> CORE -> REPORTING (+ <CANVAS>_BI views)
   '-- Postgres slices (validation only): landing -> staging -> core -> reporting
                 |
        Jaspersoft domains (Standard Offering, one org and datasource per client)
                 |
        JasperReports Server: Origin_DEV -> Origin_TEST -> client test orgs -> client prod orgs
                 |
        Client users: Ad Hoc views, dashboards, standardized reports, scheduled CSV / PDF (SFTP, email)
```

Two repositories: `originba_dbt` owns the data layer (models, the Oracle-native build, nightly QA, the
client registry `clients.yml`, generated domains); `OriginBA-3` owns delivery (hand-built domains,
report packs, promotion tools, the snapshot layer SQL, the portal). Everything client-side is reached
over the VPN from one Mac; nothing writes to a client's production without `--i-mean-prod`.

## 1. Client database side

**Registry.** `clients.yml` is the one client list: org, datasource name, PDB, Oracle version, thread
count, prod and dev instances. `scripts/client_registry.py` validates it and exports the portal and
Jaspersoft copies. Credentials are env key families named there; values live only in `~/OriginBA-3/.env`.

**Onboarding runbook** (skill `oracle-native-rollout`): 1 registry row; 2 env keys with the PDB named
exactly; 3 capacity gate (size the warehouse from `all_tables`, cap TEMP before the first build; the
246 GB TEMP incident is why; still manual SQL, the tool is on the roadmap); 4 drift check
`scripts/oracle_source_drift.py`, then `scripts/create_source_compat_views.py` only where columns
differ; 5 schemas and grants (`scripts/setup_intdev_warehouse.py` is the model); 6 build both paths;
7 merge proofs `scripts/test_oracle_merge.py` (idempotence) and `test_oracle_mutation.py`
(propagation); 8 reconcile per `client-slice-validation`, arrears against `CMS_SA_SNAPSHOT` at 100%;
9 `scripts/create_bi_views.py` and the generated domains, one Ad Hoc query; 10 schedule.

**The build.** `scripts/build_oracle_warehouse.sh` is the only entry point: preflight
(`preflight_oracle_build.py`: no dbt already running, no orphan sessions, no stranded backups), compile
the Postgres project, `generate_oracle_project.py` (sqlglot transpile and lint), then `dbt build` on the
generated tree; `--staged` runs the heavy tier single-threaded. Measured on Ellensburg 25.4 (5.39M
FTs): incremental 11m12s, full refresh 33m6s, merge pass under a minute.

**Nightly** (`scripts/nightly_oracle_build.sh <client>`, launchd every 6 h: INT_DEV 05:45/11:45/17:45/23:45,
Ellensburg 00:30/06:30/12:30/18:30; the overnight fire is the full refresh): lock, VPN gate (skip and
alert when down), build, BI views, canary, source parity (`qa_specs/oracle_source_parity.yml`, 121
checks, ledger per client), snapshot parity (`oracle_snapshot_parity.yml`, 8 checks against the
`*_RPT_CURR` tables, ROWSCN stability), scorecard and fleet pages, `qa_alert.py` on red.

**Weekly** (`scripts/weekly_qa.sh`, Monday 07:00): fixtures both paths, incremental equivalence, five
client slices, DQ rules (22), schema drift at six clients, a rotating client reconciliation, the
INT_DEV full extract and publish, the Oracle-native merge and mutation proofs, the tenant readiness
sweep, `regenerate_all.sh`.

## 2. The dbt layer

153 staging views, 61 core models, 38 reporting canvases (`models/`), contracts enforced from
`models/marts/reporting/_reporting.yml`, the data dictionary and column lineage generated by
`scripts/regenerate_all.sh` (contracts, dictionary, portal catalog, generated domains, explorer packs,
scripts index). CI (`.github/workflows/ci.yml`) builds both paths on Postgres 13 and 16 per pull
request; CD (`deploy.yml`) is Slim CI per client warehouse on merge to main.

## 3. Jaspersoft domains

Generated: `scripts/generate_jaspersoft_domains.py` from the reporting contracts into
`jaspersoft/domains/<target>/` (9 per target, 1,476 items), byte-identical on rebuild
(`scripts/test_repo_structure.py`), bound through the `<CANVAS>_BI` views. Hand-built (the delivered
Standard Offering): `OriginBA-3/domains/manual_imports/*` with a builder each on
`scripts/jaspersoft/domain_schema.py`, validated by `validate_domain_schema.py`, created with
`jrs_import_domain.py` (packaged with the org's own datasource, metadata and probe query), changed in
place with `jrs_domain_patch_apply.py` under the additive-only guard (`domain_schema.additions_only`)
with seven proofs and `--restore`.

## 4. Snapshot layer (what production reads today)

`OriginBA-3/sql/performance/snapshots/deployment_steps` 01 to 13 per client: tables, procedures,
one-time baselines with a ready gate, validation gates (no empty tables, no duplicate keys, FT
parity), rolling-window updates, operational refreshes, `DBMS_SCHEDULER` jobs (twice daily with a
30-minute stagger since 2026-09-18), run-capture, BSEG flow, data-quality checks.
`scripts/local/run_snapshot_rollout_step.py --step <name> --clients <c>` runs them and logs under
`deploy/snapshot_rollout_logs/`. Long refreshes go through `RUN_JOB(use_current_session=>FALSE)`.

## 5. Jaspersoft delivery

Servers: test (JRS 10.0, eight orgs), prod (10.0 since 2026-09-21, five client orgs), internal.
Folder: `/SmartCity/Report/Standard_Offering/<workstream>` plus `Standardized_Reports`. Path:
author on Origin_DEV; Origin_DEV to Origin_TEST and to client test orgs with
`run_client_import_pipeline.py` (fresh datasource overlay, two verifiers, `rootTenantId`); client test
to client prod with `jrs_promote_test_to_prod.py` (prod's own datasource, refuses a surviving test
host, `--i-mean-prod`); any resource between any orgs with `jrs_promote.py` as root (package from
the target's own datasource and domains, import, re-export compare, execute). Before and after:
`jrs_inventory.py snapshot` and `diff`; `jrs_run_sweep.py` executes every view, report and dashboard;
`scripts/doc/build_standard_offering_validation_doc.py` writes the sign-off docx from the sweep.
Reports: `generate_sql_report_pack.py` (JRXML 7 from specs, tests) and `jrs_deploy_report_units.py`
(REST, rendered as proof). Scheduling is a JRS job per report (`jrs_repository.py jobs`, `jrs_get.py`).

## 6. Where AI sits

Claude Code sessions run the tools above on instruction, in the order the skills prescribe, and stop
on a failed proof; the skills (`.claude/skills` in both repos) hold the runbooks, the measured facts
and the dated lessons, updated in the same session a fact is learned. Guardrails are in the tools:
`--confirm <Org>` on every write, `--i-mean-prod` on prod, a read-only Oracle MCP with prod disabled,
credential values never printed. Since February 2026 Claude co-authored 303 of 379 commits in
OriginBA-3 and 255 of 351 in originba_dbt.

## What the automation achieved (measured)

- `fct_billed_charge` from 31 minutes and 246 GB of TEMP (failed) to 21 seconds; BODA staging 1,898 s to
  6.8 s; `rpt_unbilled_revenue` 14 minutes to 4 seconds.
- 120 of 120 parity checks across 38 tables; 138,086 of 138,086 SA balances to the cent against the
  client's own snapshot.
- Standard Offering to prod in 11 minutes (Ellensburg) and 21 (Newark1) with 0 errors; 111 views
  repaired the morning prod went 10.0; the Adjustment domain exact on six client orgs; 21 College
  Station reports moved prod to test byte-equal.

## Where it needs to improve

- The nightlies run from one Mac over the VPN: 93% of the last 30 days' runs were skipped; the CD
  workflow has failed 13 of 13 times (no reachable warehouse from GitHub). The fix is a runner inside
  the VCN (OKE CronJob); designed, not built.
- Two reporting layers: clients read the snapshot tables; the dbt warehouse is live only on INT_DEV and
  Ellensburg test; generated domains are not yet bound on any server; other clients wait on DBA
  approval for the `ORIGINBA_*` schemas and a service account.
- No capacity-gate script; Standard Offering on prod for three of five clients; standardized reports
  not yet schedulable with relative dates; the snapshot schedule is documented three different ways.

## Roadmap

1 runner in the VCN; 2 capacity-gate tool; 3 first client onboarded on the in-database warehouse;
4 standardized reports (interactive tables, as-of reports, relative dates); 5 Standard Offering on
every prod org with domains bound to the warehouse.
